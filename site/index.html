Welcome to Higher Computing Science

Introduction

Welcome to Higher Computing Science.

Higher Computing Science builds on the knowledge and skills developed at National 5 and introduces more advanced concepts in software development, computer systems, and database design.

Throughout the course, you will develop your ability to analyse problems, design solutions, write programs, and evaluate the effectiveness of your work. You will also learn how computer systems operate and how data can be stored, organised, and processed efficiently.

Computing Science is a practical subject that combines problem-solving, logical thinking, creativity, and technical knowledge. The skills developed during this course are highly valued in a wide range of careers including software development, cybersecurity, data science, artificial intelligence, engineering, finance, and digital technologies.

Course Structure

The Higher Computing Science course is divided into three main areas:

Software Design and Development

In this unit, you will learn how to:

  • analyse problems and identify requirements
  • design solutions using recognised design techniques
  • develop programs using appropriate programming constructs
  • test and evaluate software solutions
  • improve the efficiency and maintainability of programs

Computer Systems

In this unit, you will learn about:

  • computer architecture
  • processor performance
  • memory and storage
  • networking and the internet
  • cybersecurity and data security
  • environmental and ethical issues related to computing

Database Design and Development

In this unit, you will learn how to:

  • design relational databases
  • identify entities, attributes, and relationships
  • use primary and foreign keys
  • create and interpret SQL queries
  • extract and manipulate data efficiently

Course Assessment

The Higher Computing Science qualification is assessed through a question paper and an assignment.

Assessment Component Marks Weighting
Question Paper 110 73%
Assignment 40 27%
Total 150 100%

The question paper assesses knowledge, understanding, and problem-solving skills across all areas of the course.

The assignment gives you the opportunity to apply your knowledge and skills to solve a practical computing problem and produce a working solution.

Skills You Will Develop

By studying Higher Computing Science, you will develop:

  • computational thinking skills
  • problem-solving skills
  • programming skills
  • analytical skills
  • database design skills
  • logical reasoning skills
  • evaluation and testing skills
  • independent learning skills

These skills are transferable and can support further study, apprenticeships, university courses, and employment in a wide range of sectors.

Success in Higher Computing Science

Success in Higher Computing Science comes from regular practice. Reading notes alone is rarely enough. You should aim to:

  • complete programming tasks regularly
  • practise exam-style questions
  • review worked examples
  • test and debug your own solutions
  • develop confidence in explaining your thinking

The more experience you gain solving problems, the more confident you will become when tackling unfamiliar challenges.
